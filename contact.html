Service Area

We serve the entire Twin Cities metro area, including:

  • Minneapolis and Saint Paul
  • Hennepin, Ramsey, Anoka, Dakota, and Washington Counties
  • Surrounding suburbs within a 40-mile radius of downtown Minneapolis

Our transport program also partners with rural shelters throughout greater Minnesota. If you live outside our service area and want to foster or adopt, contact us and we will do our best to find a solution.
