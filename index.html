Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a volunteer-powered, community nonprofit that rescues, rehabilitates, and rehomes companion animals across the Twin Cities metro area. We believe no animal should be left behind because of age, breed, or medical need, and we work with local families, veterinarians, and partners to give every pet a safe place to land.

Our Impact

Since opening our doors, the community has helped us reach milestones we could not have imagined alone.

  • 1,240 animals adopted into permanent homes
  • 310 active foster families opening their homes each year
  • 85 volunteers contributing more than 9,000 hours annually
  • 97% live-release rate across dogs, cats, and small animals
  • 22 partner veterinarians and businesses supporting our work

How You Can Help

There is a role for everyone at Twin Cities Animal Rescue, whether you have a spare room, a spare hour, or a spare few dollars.

  • Adopt. Give a rescued pet a permanent home. Learn about the adoption process.
  • Foster. Provide temporary care while an animal waits for a family. Explore the foster program.
  • Volunteer. Walk dogs, socialize cats, staff events, or help with transport. See volunteer roles.
  • Donate. Your gift funds vaccines, surgeries, food, and shelter for animals in our care.
