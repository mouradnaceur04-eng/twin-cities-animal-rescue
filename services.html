Adoption Process

Adopting from Twin Cities Animal Rescue is designed to be thoughtful but not intimidating. Our goal is a match that lasts a lifetime.

  1. Browse and inquire. Meet our available pets online or at a weekend adoption event, then submit an interest form through our Contact page.
  2. Meet and greet. An adoption counselor arranges a visit so you and the animal can spend time together, including any current pets in your home.
  3. Home conversation. We talk through your schedule, living space, and expectations so we can set both you and the pet up for success.
  4. Finalize. Sign the adoption agreement and pay the adoption fee, which covers spay or neuter surgery, vaccinations, and microchipping.
  5. Ongoing support. Our team stays available for questions after adoption, from training tips to veterinary referrals.

Foster Program

Fostering is the backbone of our rescue. Every animal in a foster home is one more animal we can say yes to. We provide the food, supplies, and veterinary care; you provide the couch and the patience.

Who We Need Fosters For

  • Puppies and kittens too young for adoption
  • Animals recovering from surgery or illness
  • Seniors who do better in a calm home than a kennel
  • Shy or under-socialized pets learning to trust people

What Fostering Involves

  • Commitments range from two weeks to several months
  • Basic daily care, socialization, and transport to vet visits
  • Short updates and photos to help the animal get adopted
  • A foster coordinator on call for questions and emergencies

Apply to foster and we will match you with an animal that fits your household.

Volunteer Roles

Volunteers keep our doors open. Most roles require a one-time orientation and a minimum of four hours per month. Volunteers must be 16 or older, or 12 and older with a parent.

Dog Walker
Provide daily exercise and enrichment for dogs in our care. Ideal for early risers and weekend regulars.
Cat Socializer
Spend quiet time with cats and kittens to build confidence and prepare them for adoption.
Adoption Event Helper
Set up, greet visitors, and answer questions at our weekend adoption events across the metro area.
Transport Driver
Drive animals to veterinary appointments, foster homes, and partner shelters. A reliable vehicle and clean driving record are required.
Photographer and Storyteller
Take photos and write short bios that help our animals stand out online.
Community Outreach Ambassador
Represent the rescue at fairs, schools, and local businesses to spread the word about adoption and fostering.

Sign up to volunteer and tell us which role interests you.
