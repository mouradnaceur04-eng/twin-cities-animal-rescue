Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue started in 2014 in a single garage in Northeast Minneapolis. A handful of neighbors were fostering stray cats through a hard winter and realized that, working together, they could do far more than any of them could alone. Within a year they had formed a nonprofit, recruited their first volunteers, and placed their first hundred animals.

Today we operate a small adoption center, a network of more than 300 foster homes, and a transport program that brings animals from overcrowded rural shelters into the metro area, where adopters are waiting. We remain proudly volunteer-led, and every dollar donated goes toward the animals in our care.

Adoption changes two lives at once. Our adopters tell us again and again that the pet they rescued rescued them right back.

Our Core Values

Compassion First
Every animal is treated as an individual with a name, a history, and a future, never as a number.
Transparency
We publish our intake, adoption, and financial figures every year so donors and partners can see exactly how their support is used.
Community Ownership
This rescue belongs to the neighbors who built it. We listen to our volunteers, fosters, and adopters when we make decisions.
No Animal Left Behind
Age, breed, and medical needs are never reasons to turn an animal away. We find a path for every pet that comes through our door.
Trust and Follow-Through
When we say we will call, we call. When we say a pet is healthy, it is. We earn trust one honest interaction at a time.

Our Partners

We are grateful to the local businesses, clinics, and organizations whose support keeps our doors open and our animals healthy.

  • Northside Veterinary Clinic provides discounted spay, neuter, and emergency care.
  • Lakeside Pet Supply donates food, litter, and bedding for our foster network.
  • Mississippi Bend Community Foundation funds our transport and medical assistance programs.
  • Riverview Print and Design prints our adoption event materials at no cost.
  • Twin Cities Dog Training Collective offers free classes to new adopters.
  • Local rural shelters across greater Minnesota partner with us to transfer animals who need more time and space to find homes.

Interested in becoming a partner? Reach out through our Contact page and select "Community partnership" as your interest type.
